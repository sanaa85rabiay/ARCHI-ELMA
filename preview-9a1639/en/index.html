The firm

Architectural expertise,
at the service of your projects

ARCHI-ELMA is an architecture firm founded and led by El Maaiti Abdallah, a licensed architect. Based in the heart of Technopole 2 Agadir Bay, the firm guides individuals and businesses in bringing their construction, extension and renovation projects to life.

Our conviction: a good project starts with listening. We take the time to understand your land, your needs and your budget, in order to design spaces that truly feel like you — beautiful, functional and built to last.
