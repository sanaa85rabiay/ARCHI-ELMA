About ARCHI-ELMA

A human-scale architecture firm, rooted in Agadir and driven by excellence.

El Maaiti Abdallah

Architecture is listening before it is drawing

Founder of the ARCHI-ELMA firm, El Maaiti Abdallah is a licensed architect. Since establishing his practice at Technopole 2 Agadir Bay, he has been putting his expertise at the service of individuals and businesses across the Agadir region.

Family villas, office buildings, complex renovations: every commission receives the same attention — understanding the land, respecting the budget, refining the detail. This quiet rigour has built the firm's reputation, from Agadir Bay to Ouled Teima and Biougra.

From the first sketch to the handover of the keys, you work with a single point of contact, available and committed for the entire duration of your project.
